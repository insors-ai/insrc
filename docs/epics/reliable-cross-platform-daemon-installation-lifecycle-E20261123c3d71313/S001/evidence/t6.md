No PID, lease-age, endpoint-disappearance or journal-only inference can authorize a new runner.

Migration requires the natural-exit receipt, released ownership and exact ordered hook-test journal evidence, including empty-hook owner confirmation. Explicit recovery reuses validated prior completion, or needs positive owning-hook retry policy after observed failed child exit; held/unknown ownership or missing/mismatched evidence remains blocked. Activation requires exact ready installation/release/instance/session/inventory/endpoint/nonce. Selection commits atomically only afterward. Before-disruption failures preserve incumbent selection/running intent; post-disruption uncertainty stays blocked without automatic predecessor restart. Candidate files are revalidated after drain and before launch.
