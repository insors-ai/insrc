## 1. Problem

People cannot reliably account for the model work consumed while creating and reviewing workflow artifacts. A provider response may contain usage figures, but those figures alone do not establish a durable, attributable history through retries, corrections, interrupted runs and artifact rewrites. Without a shared accounting boundary, later capture and delivery features can lose consumed work, count overlapping observations twice, misattribute shared or reviewed work, or present missing measurement as zero, leaving task, story and epic explanations untrustworthy.

## 2. Functional requirements

- **E20261026c66d49bb:FR001** — Retain immutable, attributable usage facts for workflow artifacts, including the model, step/run, request/attempt, activity and measurement provenance.
- **E20261026c66d49bb:FR002** — Select one trustworthy effective observation for consumed work while retaining correction, reconciliation and ownership history, and disclose unresolved accounting conflicts.
- **E20261026c66d49bb:FR003** — Persist root and descendant records in one recoverable Avro ledger per canonical root, including concurrent appends and reserved identities before artifact content exists.
- **E20261026c66d49bb:FR004** — Keep artifact JSON and usage persistence independent through lifecycle changes, paired import/export, retention and binary-safe branch reconciliation.
- **E20261026c66d49bb:FR005** — Provide daemon-owned artifact-level accounting access and explicit compatibility, coverage and operational outcomes for later producers and read models.

- **E20261026c66d49bb:S001:FR002** — Resolve observations conservatively while preserving every original fact and disclosing uncertainty, conflicts and explicit ownership changes.

## 3. Non-goals

- **Instrument workflow or provider inference dispatch sites, or implement native Claude/Codex/Ollama usage extraction.** — TU2 owns automatic capture and provider normalization. TU1 qualifies measurement boundaries as contract evidence and supplies the foundation it consumes.
- **Implement controller-host reporting integrations or new controller-facing MCP telemetry flows.** — TU3 owns host capability negotiation, reporting and host integrations; TU1 supplies shared event/source semantics and daemon-owned accounting access.
- **Compute task/story/epic subtree totals or add usage to DeliverySnapshot and board UI.** — TU4 owns work hierarchy read models and TU5 owns client presentation. TU1 reduces observations for accounting without building those products.
- **Replace the generic common artifact store, lifecycle, journal or artifact identity scheme.** — Reuse the common identity, ownership and lifecycle contracts. Usage persistence is a dedicated external root-ledger service; it does not replace generic artifact content storage.
- **Add monetary pricing, invoice reconciliation, budgets, inference interception, automatic model downgrades or raw prompt/response retention.** — The PRD scope excludes invoice reconciliation, pricing/budgets, automatic model downgrades, general traffic interception and prompt/completion retention.

## 6. Stories

### 6.1 STORY-c66d49bb81925886-s1 — Distinguish and reconcile immutable usage observations

**User value:** `size: L`

An accounting consumer can tell which model work was observed, what remains unknown, and which observation contributes without double counting corrections or overlap.

**Extends:** [[c11]]

**Acceptance criteria:**

- **ac1:** Given usage evidence for retries of delivery and for genuinely separate model attempts, when the evidence is accepted and interpreted, then replayed delivery identifies the same event, actual attempts remain separate, and each attempt has one charged owner plus workflow-step/run and source/model provenance. _(operationalizes `k1`, `k2`, `k7`)_
- **ac2:** Given reported counters, estimates, unavailable counters, pending attempts and legacy absence, when a consumer reads their meaning, then reported zero is distinct from every missing/estimated state, cache buckets do not overlap, reasoning is not added again, and unsafe or invalid counts cannot become trusted usage. _(operationalizes `k4`, `k9`)_
- **ac3:** Given a corrected observation or aggregate evidence overlapping observed constituent calls, when the effective accounting contribution is requested, then only the accepted non-overlapping observation basis contributes, original facts remain available, and unsupported or unreconciled overlap is explicitly unresolved rather than silently added. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k3`, `k4`)_
- **ac4:** Given duplicate identities with different payloads or correction/reconciliation references that fork, cycle or do not resolve, when the evidence is interpreted, then the conflict is visible and untrusted branches cannot silently replace known facts or increase a claimed complete total. _(operationalizes `k3`, `k9`)_
- **ac5:** Given dispatch-time attribution followed by an explicit change of ownership, when the accounting history is interpreted, then the effective owner is unambiguous, the prior owner and reason remain traceable, and the same consumption is not charged to both owners. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k3`, `k7`)_
- **ac6:** Given qualified evidence about what a native or external source can expose, when the accounting contract describes that source, then per-call observations, invocation aggregates, cumulative observations and unknown physical-call counts remain distinguishable without requiring TU2/TU3 instrumentation to exist. _(operationalizes `k4`, `k10`)_

### 6.2 STORY-c66d49bb81925886-s2 — Persist root and descendant usage in a recoverable Avro ledger

**User value:** `size: L`

A producer can rely on one root usage file retaining all acknowledged root and child activity, even before artifact content exists.

**Depends on:** `s1`

**Extends:** [[c11]]

**Acceptance criteria:**

- **ac1:** Given a usage submission associated with an existing artifact, when the service acknowledges durable acceptance and then restarts, then the complete accepted Avro event remains readable exactly once in the canonical root file with its original event, root and charged-artifact identities. _(operationalizes `k1`, `k2`, `k6`)_
- **ac2:** Given simultaneous root/child submissions, interruption during block/header/directory persistence, or corruption in committed data, when recovery and replay complete, then acknowledged blocks survive, incomplete tails are detected/recovered, interior corruption is surfaced, identical replay adds no charge and conflicting identity reuse remains visible. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k3`, `k6`)_
- **ac3:** Given usage targeted at a stable intended artifact identity before valid artifact content exists, when the producer records usage and the artifact is subsequently created, then the evidence survives in the reserved root ledger and attribution resolves once without copying records into artifact JSON, fabricating content or approval, or retaining a second final ledger. _(operationalizes `k1`, `k6`)_
- **ac4:** Given a durable start without a final usable observation, when the service recovers after interruption, then the attempt is represented as pending/interrupted with unavailable usage rather than successful measured zero. _(operationalizes `k4`, `k6`)_
- **ac5:** Given a persistence failure before start acknowledgment or after a model result was obtained, when the producer receives the accounting outcome, then it can distinguish durable acceptance from failure/pending recovery, retry recording safely, and is never instructed to rerun inference to repair accounting. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k6`)_

### 6.3 STORY-c66d49bb81925886-s3 — Keep usage independent of artifact lifecycle and reconcile ledger transfers

**User value:** `size: L`

A reviewer retains both a trustworthy usage history and the approved content state while normal artifact operations continue.

**Depends on:** `s2`

**Extends:** [[c20]]

**Acceptance criteria:**

- **ac1:** Given an approved or signed-off artifact with recorded usage, when only usage facts are appended, then root usage freshness advances while artifact JSON bytes, content hash/revision, approval and signoff remain unchanged. _(operationalizes `k5`)_
- **ac2:** Given an artifact with an external root usage ledger, when it undergoes supported rewriting, regeneration, content revision, lifecycle updates or kind-specific serialization and round-trip import/export, then ordinary content operations leave ledger bytes intact; paired import/export retains usage or explicitly discloses missing ledger history. _(operationalizes `k1`, `k5`)_
- **ac3:** Given rejected, cancelled or superseded work that consumed model usage, when new content or a successor artifact is recorded, then the consumed history remains attributable and is not erased or copied into a second charge. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k5`, `k9`)_
- **ac4:** Given independent branch histories containing distinct events and an identical replay, when the histories are reconciled, then daemon-mediated binary-safe import retains both distinct events once, conflicting IDs remain explicit and no ordinary text merge or timestamp chooses a winner. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k3`, `k5`)_
- **ac5:** Given an older artifact with no ledger or a ledger with an unsupported writer/record schema, when normal artifact read/write operations occur, then content remains usable, artifact writers leave ledger bytes intact and accounting reports not-recorded or unsupported rather than complete zero. _(operationalizes `k5`, `k9`)_
- **ac6:** Given an explicit ownership transfer across canonical roots interrupted between durable steps, when recovery and reporting complete, then the original charged event remains auditable, transfer references reconcile once and neither duplicate charging nor a silent gap is presented as complete. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k3`, `k5`, `k9`)_

### 6.4 STORY-c66d49bb81925886-s4 — Provide daemon accounting access and operational guarantees

**User value:** `size: M`

Later capture and read-model features can use a scoped, documented accounting service without implementing their own store or relying on an unfinished board.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- **ac2:** Given invalid identity, wrong scope, unresolved ownership or a caller attempting to target arbitrary files, when the request reaches accounting access, then it is rejected or explicitly held unresolved without modifying an unrelated artifact or inventing attribution. _(operationalizes `k2`, `k3`, `k7`, `k8`)_
- **ac3:** Given a retained history large enough to require bounded reading, when a consumer reads successive portions at a recorded usage watermark, then it can traverse the acknowledged history consistently without silent omission, while growth/retention outcomes and any limits are explicit. _(operationalizes `k6`, `k9`, `k10`)_
- **ac4:** Given a recovery backlog, unsupported evidence or a retention/merge problem, when the service is inspected or retried, then the unresolved state and recoverable outcome are visible, no acknowledged history is silently truncated, and content approval semantics stay independent. _(operationalizes `k3`, `k5`, `k6`, `k9`)_
- **ac5:** Given the accounting foundation and its documented producer/preservation contracts, when its acceptance fixtures run without provider capture, host reporting, hierarchical delivery queries or board code, then the foundation is complete and consumable, and no out-of-scope product capability or weaker inference policy is introduced. _(operationalizes `k8`, `k10`)_
- **ac6:** Given the agreed first-release root-file scale and physical Avro schema, when library, schema evolution, paging and committed-snapshot qualification run, then build acceptance records measured size/read/recovery bounds and demonstrates decoding the chosen Avro schema through a committed snapshot and the existing converter or explicitly provisioned DuckDB reader; this compatibility is not claimed to exist before implementation. _(operationalizes `k6`, `k9`, `k10`)_
