Filters. Exactly five: four views (All work, Epics, Standalone, Issues) as one single-choice control, and Needs attention as a toggle that applies on top of any of them. Epics are never chips; one is chosen on the Epics screen (2).

Stages. Six collapsible sections stacked vertically, in workflow order, each with its count. Non-empty stages start open; empty ones start closed with "nothing at this stage", but stay listed. Cards inside a section wrap into a grid as wide as the pane allows.

Large stages. A stage holding many cards (Complete is usually the largest) starts closed and says how many need attention, so the screen leads with work in progress.

Cards. Kicker (kind · compact id), title, epic or "Standalone", size and task results when recorded, then at most the badges that matter (review blocked, decision due, validation conflict). A card opens its item's own screen (7 or 11); the board is not shown beneath it.

Freshness. "Updated just now" sits in the app bar; Refresh re-reads. The breadcrumb is just "Delivery" at the top level.

Ready columns. The two Ready stages are alternatives (with and without a plan step), kept as two sections as the PRD requires.

Where they appear. Empty and unavailable (no snapshot at all) replace the whole body. No matches replaces the list area of the current screen, under the filters, and its action clears search and Needs attention but keeps the view. Refresh failed and partial data sit as a banner above the current screen's content, which stays usable.

Never blank. Each state says what was read, what was not, and what to do. An unavailable daemon with no earlier snapshot shows "The delivery board is unavailable" with Retry, never an empty board.
