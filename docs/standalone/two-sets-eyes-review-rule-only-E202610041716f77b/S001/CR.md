| MED | src/mcp/code-review-step/handler.ts:686 | Correctness risk under daemon version skew. `reviewByDaemon` trusts the `done` frame without checking it: it hard-codes `reviewedBy: 'daemon'` and never verifies that `res.artifact.body.groundingMode` equals the `mode` it asked for. `codeReview.run` existed before this Story, so an older daemon accepts the request, silently ignores `groundingMode: 'degraded'`, grounds on the stale or empty graph, does not cap the verdict at warn and does not stamp `meta.reviewedBy`.
