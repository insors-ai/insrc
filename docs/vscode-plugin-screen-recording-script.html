The audit story

For healthcare and finance audiences, frame this as a software change-control demonstration: what was requested, who or what produced the evidence, what was approved, what changed, and what validation supports completion. TaskGarden is a synthetic example, not a clinical or financial system.

Claim boundary Demonstrate traceability evidence. Do not claim regulatory certification, immutable audit storage, signed human identity, or guaranteed data residency. Local embeddings do not imply local reasoning. The final audit chapter explains the observed evidence and its limits.

Long reviews · trigger, pause, resume

Use this capture pattern for every required design review (DEF/HLD/LLD) and post-build code review. Keep the review running normally while recording is paused. Review duration is not demo runtime.
