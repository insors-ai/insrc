Production approach

  1. Rehearse with the actual VS Code extension and daemon build. Source snapshot reviewed 5 November 2026; extension manifest 0.5.24. This is not proof of Marketplace availability.
  2. Capture prerequisites, settings, the renewal analyzer trace, and parallel chats before the workflow chapters. Film the ERD during design review, before implementation. Budget roughly 50–65 edited minutes; individual chapter timings are targets, not promises about model latency.
  3. Capture each workflow decision at its real checkpoint. Record the delivery board’s opening and later completion shots into that chapter’s clip, pausing capture between checkpoints if needed.
  4. Hold important evidence for 4–7 seconds, keep text readable, and leave a clean lead-in/out. Shorten waits in editing while preserving errors and actual outcomes.
  5. Use synthetic catalog records only. Hide credentials, account identifiers, private repo content, notifications, and unrelated chats before capture.

The audit story

For healthcare and finance audiences, frame this as a software change-control demonstration: what was requested, who or what produced the evidence, what was approved, what changed, and what validation supports completion. The recording uses synthetic library records, not a clinical or financial system.

Claim boundary Demonstrate traceability evidence. Do not claim regulatory certification, immutable audit storage, signed human identity, or guaranteed data residency. Local embeddings do not imply local reasoning. The final audit chapter explains the observed evidence and its limits.
