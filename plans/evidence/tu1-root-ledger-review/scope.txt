Changing workflow stages, review verdicts or approvals because of usage volume. Coverage boundary: work executed by an external controller may be invisible to the daemon. The controller must submit usage through the same capture contract where its host exposes it. Missing host counters are marked unavailable. No integration may claim complete coverage merely because all daemon calls were measured.
